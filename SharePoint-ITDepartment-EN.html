One wrong click can lock everyone out of the CRM

Three habits, ten seconds each — that's all it takes to stay safe:

Before you click

  • Hover the link. Unusual URL? Ask IT, don't click.
  • Check the actual sender address, not the display name.
  • Urgent payment or "account locked" email? Call to confirm first.

Passwords

  • If "IT" emails asking for your password — that's not us.
  • Don't reuse your work password anywhere else.
  • Login prompt you didn't trigger? Deny it, change your password.

See something unusual? Forward to itsupport@esadservices.com. 10 seconds.
